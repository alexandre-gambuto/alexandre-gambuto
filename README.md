# Alexandre Gambuto

Licensing & Business Development Manager in **technology transfer** and **intellectual property**, based in Rennes, France.

*En français : chargé d'affaires en transfert de technologie et propriété intellectuelle, à Rennes.*

## What I do

- Bring technologies from public research to industry: patent and software licensing,
  and the agreements that go with them.
- Business development around intellectual property: finding industrial partners and
  structuring the deals.
- On my own time, build the productivity tools this work calls for, mostly in VBA for
  Outlook and Excel.

## Projects

- **[ClassicOutlook-Toolkit](https://github.com/alexandre-gambuto/ClassicOutlook-Toolkit)**:
  an open-source productivity suite for Outlook Classic, in pure VBA. Mail filing, project
  tagging of appointments, reply tracking, mail campaigns, contacts and analytics. Zero
  install, works on locked-down workstations. GPL-3.0.
  Project page: [English](https://alexandre-gambuto.com/classicoutlook-toolkit-en/) ·
  [français](https://alexandre-gambuto.com/classicoutlook-toolkit/)
